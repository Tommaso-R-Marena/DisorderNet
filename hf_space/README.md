# What can your benchmark resolve?

Computes the number of methods a benchmark can place in a certified order from
its annotation error rate, how many of its comparisons are undecidable, and what
a statistic with the pairing structure would buy.

Backed by [`disordernet`](https://pypi.org/project/disordernet/) and by a Lean 4
development that is `sorry`-free on the standard axioms.
