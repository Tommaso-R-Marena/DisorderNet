**The protocol.** `scoredPairs` are the ordered pairs of distinct residues in the same group; a labelling returns on such a pair the verdict "the first is disordered and the second is not" (`Verdict`); `pairDiscord` counts the scored pairs on which the annotation's verdict differs from the truth's, and `ν_pair = pairDiscord / #scoredPairs`.

**1. `pairwise_capacity`.** A family of methods certified at the pair level has at most `⌈1/(2·ν_pair)⌉` members (formally `max 1 ⌈1/(2 ν_pair)⌉`, the `max 1` being the degenerate one-method case, as in the existing capacity results). It is obtained by identifying the noise of the induced pairwise answer key with the pair discordance (`noise_pairLabel`) and instantiating the existing capacity machinery at the type of scored pairs, so the pairwise protocol obeys the same law as the residue protocol with its own rate substituted.

**2. `correlated_noise_reduces_pair_discordance`.** Hypotheses: the truth is constant on the blocks of a partition into blocks of `b` residues, the error set `T Δ L` is also a union of blocks (noise flips whole blocks), and each block carries at most `D` boundary-crossing scored pairs. Conclusion:

  ν_pair ≤ (boundary mass / total scored pairs) · ν_label,  boundary mass = D · #blocks, ν_label = |T Δ L| / #residues.

The mechanism is isolated as `discordPairs_subset`: a scored pair can be corrupted only if it straddles a block boundary *and* touches a flipped block — both endpoints of a within-block pair carry equal labels under the truth and under the annotation, so its verdict is unchanged whether or not the block flipped. The integer form `card_mul_pairDiscord_le` (`#residues · corrupted pairs ≤ D · #blocks · mislabelled residues`) is deterministic: nothing is assumed about which blocks flip or how many. The `ν_label` factor comes from `card_blockConstant` — a block-constant error set of size `|T Δ L|` is exactly `|T Δ L|/b` flipped blocks.

**3. The order of magnitude.** On a chain compared in adjacent pairs with noise blocks of `b` consecutive residues: a block is cut only at its two ends, so carries at most four boundary-crossing ordered pairs (`chain_boundaryDeg_le_four`), there are `n/b` blocks and at least `n` scored pairs, giving `chain_pairwise_noise_bound : ν_pair ≤ (4/b)·ν_label` — the boundary fraction is O(1/|block|). Equivalently `chain_capacity_ceiling_gain : 1/(2 ν_pair) ≥ (b/4)·1/(2 ν_label)`, so the ceiling grows linearly in the block length. `chain_aligned_no_discordance` gives the extreme case: when the blocks are unions of comparison groups (even block length here) the protocol is exactly noise-free, `ν_pair = 0`, however many blocks flip. `chain_worked_instance` verifies by decision procedure that the hypotheses are satisfiable non-trivially: six residues, blocks of three, the second block flipped — `ν_label = 1/2` while `ν_pair = 1/6`, the single pair cut by the boundary.

Scope note: these are bounds on the rates and hence on the capacity ceilings; a larger ceiling is permission to order more methods, not a proof that a given leaderboard does. `D · #blocks` is an upper bound for the total boundary mass, tight when it is spread evenly over blocks, as on a homogeneous chain.
