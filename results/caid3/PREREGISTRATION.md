# Pre-registration — windowed-training run on CAID3

> **CONCLUDED.** This registration governed the windowed run and its outcome is
> in `CORRECTED.md`: P1 confirmed at adjusted p=0.0252, P2 failed, non-inferiority
> passed. Its floor of 0.9553 is history and is deliberately left as written —
> rewriting it would erase what that run was actually held to. The live
> registration is `PREREGISTRATION_2.md`, whose floors come from corrected
> measurements.


Written and committed **before** the run starts and before any of its results
exist. The git history is the timestamp.

## Why this document exists

The previous checkpoint (`mt_full`, commit `7bd80d6`) scored 0.9603 on the 319
official Disorder-PDB targets against a published leader of 0.955, and beat the
training-free AlphaFold-rsa baseline at p=0.013. That p-value came from a family
of **16** comparisons run against these same references over the course of one
working session. Under Holm–Bonferroni across that family it adjusts to 0.143 and
does not survive:

| comparison                          | p_raw | p_adj | survives |
|-------------------------------------|-------|-------|----------|
| disorder_pdb: fused − PUNCH2        | 0.006 | 0.084 | no       |
| disorder_pdb: ours − AlphaFold-rsa  | 0.013 | 0.143 | no       |
| disorder_pdb: ours − PUNCH2         | 0.205 | 1.000 | no       |

The rank is unaffected — 0.9603 > 0.955 is a point estimate and a ranking needs
no p-value — but every *significance* claim from that run is exploratory. It was
generated by looking, not by testing.

This run is the confirmatory one. The hypotheses, the test, the correction and
the success criteria are all fixed below, before the data exists.

## What changes, and only this

One variable: proteins longer than `--max-len` (1022) are kept as overlapping
windows instead of being dropped. That recovers 2,440 proteins on the
pdb_missing source alone — 11% of the training data, and the hard part of it.
Everything else is identical to `mt_full`: same tasks, same backbone (ESM-2 650M,
frozen), same `structure_dim=24`, 5 folds, 8 epochs, same seed, same leak filter.

A single change keeps the comparison interpretable. If the number moves, this is
why it moved.

## Motivation, stated in advance

On CAID3 Disorder-NOX, `mt_full`'s predecessor scored 0.5183 — chance — on the
7 targets above 1500 residues, where training-free AlphaFold-rsa scores 0.8967.
The model had never seen a protein that long in training. Windowed *inference*
alone moved that band only to 0.5521, so the deficit is discrimination, not
context window: the model has to be trained on such proteins, not merely shown
them politely at test time.

The benchmarks reward this specifically. Every CAID3 method that declines targets
declines the long ones (median length 1292 declined vs 344 overall), and the
difficulty gap on declined targets is +0.118 AUC on Disorder-NOX.

## Primary endpoints

Two, fixed in advance, on **Disorder-PDB**, **unfused**, all **319** official
targets, 100% coverage required, paired protein-clustered bootstrap
(10,000 resamples, two-sided), Holm–Bonferroni across **these two tests only**:

- **P1** — DisorderNet > AlphaFold-rsa.
  The minimum bar: AlphaFold-rsa is training-free and ranks 3rd on this
  benchmark. A trained model that cannot beat it has not earned its training.
- **P2** — DisorderNet > PUNCH2 (rank 1, published 0.955).
  A stretch. The observed margin was +0.0051 and the benchmark's resolution is
  roughly +0.003, so this is underpowered and is expected to fail. It is
  registered anyway so that a success cannot be claimed as if it had been
  planned, and a failure cannot be quietly dropped.

Family size is 2. Nothing else is added to this family afterwards.

## Non-inferiority constraint (retaining what works)

The run must not damage the existing strength. Pre-specified:

> Disorder-PDB AUC must not fall below **0.9553** — that is `mt_full`'s 0.9603
> minus a 0.005 margin, and it is also PUNCH2's published figure.

If it falls below that, the windowed variant is rejected for Disorder-PDB
regardless of what it does elsewhere, and `mt_full` remains the reported model.

## Secondary endpoints — exploratory, and labelled as such

Disorder-NOX, Linker, Binding, Binding-IDR: AUC, rank among the 117 entrants,
and paired comparison against each published leader. Reported with Holm applied
within the secondary family, and reported **whether or not** they improve.

These are explicitly not confirmatory. Three of them cannot resolve a winner at
all: the margin needed to demonstrate a win is +0.035 on Binding and +0.061 on
Linker, against leaders at 0.776 and 0.898. No current leader can prove a win
there either.

## Stopping rule

One run, one evaluation, reported once. No re-running the evaluation with
different fusion partners, bootstrap seeds, or resample counts until a p-value
crosses a threshold. If the confirmatory test fails, it failed.

## What will be reported regardless of outcome

- Every primary and secondary number, adjusted and raw.
- Coverage for every benchmark (must be 319/319 on Disorder-PDB, or the run is
  void — a subset score is what forced an earlier retraction).
- The non-inferiority check result.
- A statement of which claims are confirmatory and which are exploratory.
